# Personas

Every PRD and every PRD review uses these personas. They describe kinds of users, not real people. Add or change one only with the human's approval, like a PRD change.

## The weekend creator

- **Goal:** turn their own stories into voice dramas as a hobby, and enjoy the process.
- **Setting:** writes at a PC in the evening, and listens and reviews on a phone on the train or in bed.
- **Skills:** at home with AI content tools such as voice generators, and asks agents for things in plain words, but does not write code.
- **Fears:** surprise costs from paid voice generation, and losing an evening to setup or tools that break.
- **Success:** hears a draft of a new episode the same day they finish the script, and fixes what sounds wrong in a few taps.

## The careful publisher

- **Goal:** post finished dramas publicly, on video sites, sometimes with ads.
- **Setting:** works at a PC, and checks the final render before every upload.
- **Fears:** a sound or voice whose license does not allow the use, discovered after publishing.
- **Success:** knows, before rendering, that every sound in the drama may be used the way they plan, with the credits ready to paste.

## The agent driver

- **Goal:** let coding agents draft the scenario and generate the audio, and step in only to judge and correct.
- **Setting:** runs agents in a terminal, and reviews their output in the UI, often from a phone.
- **Fears:** agents that guess, spend money without asking, or make changes that are hard to see and undo.
- **Success:** every fix is one tap or one sentence, and they can see what the agent changed and why.
